About Me

    I’m a Mechanical Engineer passionate about troubleshooting, creating, and testing, with work experience in automotive/aerospace applications. As an undergrad at The University of Texas at Austin, I actively contribute to Longhorn Racing Combustion, and I've previously been involved in Longhorn Racing Solar, the Texas Rocket Engineering Lab, and the Longhorn Rocketry Association. I bring hands-on experience with SolidWorks, Ansys Mechanical FEA, scripting with MATLAB/Python, and fabrication techniques like 3D Printing, TIG welding, and lathing. I've also held multiple internships, working in test/validation engineering, systems engineering, and automation, with the goal of finding a full-time position that bolsters my love for blending hardware, fluids, and automation. This portfolio showcases my projects, work experience, and technical growth throughout my journey. Please view my projects and professional experience pages for a timeline of my work.
