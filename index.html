About Me

    I am a Mechanical Engineer with primary interests in engineering design, manufacturing, and troubleshooting, specifically within the automotive and aerospace sectors. As an undergraduate student at The University of Texas at Austin, I am heavily involved in various student and engineering organizations like Longhorn Racing Solar, Texas Rocket Engineering Lab, and the Longhorn Rocketry Association. I have experience in software like SolidWorks, Ansys Mechanical, and various coding languages, as well as hardware such as TIG welding and fabrication methods like lathing, manual milling, and CNC machining. This portfolio provides an overview of various work experiences, projects, and related activities that I have done since the start of my bachelor's program.
